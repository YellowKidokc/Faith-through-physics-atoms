### 🏛️ Classical Prior Art Anchors
* **Historical Provenance:** The paper's implicit epistemology is a rediscovery of themes articulated by **Charles Sanders Peirce** (fallibilism: all inquiry is provisional and revisable), **R.G. * **Who said it and why?** Peirce articulated fallibilism to explain how science progresses without certainty. Collingwood articulated the "re-enactment" model to explain how history differs from natural science. Bloch articulated the "trace" model to explain how historians work from incomplete evidence. The paper is a popular-level re-enactment of all three.
* **What makes the classical benchmark a "+8"?** The classical formulations are +8 because they are *general* (they apply to all historical knowledge, not just P52), *formal* (they can be expressed as epistemic operators), and *falsifiable* (they predict that historical knowledge will always be revisable).
