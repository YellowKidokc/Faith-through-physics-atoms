## What this channel is

Gary Habermas is a Christian philosopher and historian whose life work centers on the historical evidence for the resurrection of Jesus. The channel contains four decades of lectures, debates, Q&As, interviews, and short-form apologetics. The dominant thread is the **minimal facts argument**: a small set of resurrection data granted by most critical scholars, used as the evidential foundation for Christian belief.

Secondary threads include:
- The Shroud of Turin
- Near-death experiences (NDEs) and the afterlife
- The deity of Christ
- Gospel reliability and authorship
- Pastoral care for doubt and suffering
