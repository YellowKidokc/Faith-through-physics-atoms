## THE ARGUMENT

- TIME DIRECTION: FUTURE

**Quantum Mechanics (QM):**
- A measurement happened, here is the outcome
- The wavefunction collapsed, now we know what occurred
- Probabilistic, discrete outcomes, irreversible record
- The Born rule tells you what DID happen, not what WILL happen
- TIME DIRECTION: PAST
