The Common Sense

God is always broadcasting His grace at full strength, but how much of it reaches you depends on how much noise and pride you let get in the way. Even if the signal is clear, God never forces the door open—you have to choose to let it in.

Why the equation is built this way (The Structural Secrets)
  • The Division ($\frac{\text{Signal}}{\text{Resistance}}$): Noise drowns out the call. The more stubbornness and distraction in your denominator, the closer the received signal drops toward zero.
  • The $\log_2$ Curve: Models diminishing returns and rock-bottom breakthroughs. When you are trapped in pitch-black despair, lowering resistance just a tiny bit produces a massive, life-saving surge of light.
  • The $+1$ Inside: The mathematical safety lock. It guarantees that if you have zero signal, $\log_2(1) = 0$—so zero input cleanly yields a zero score instead of a math error.
  • Openness Out Front ($A_G \times \dots$): Your sovereign free will. If you shut the door ($\text{Openness} = 0$), it zeros out the whole product ($0 \times \text{anything} = 0$). God does not coerce.
