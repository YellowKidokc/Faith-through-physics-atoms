# Axiom Architecture — Consensus Draft

**Status:** PROPOSED CONSENSUS — NOT ADMITTED  
**Round:** Initial convergence  
**Human ruling:** Pending

## Consensus statement

The current proposals converge on the following architecture principles.

### 1. The existing Axiom paper is bundled

`02_THE_AXIOM_SPEAKS.md` presently combines claims about:

- the admitted root;
- God's identity;
- personhood and Trinity;
- aseity and divine nature;
- love, truth, and the Fruits;
- consciousness;
- divine speech and revelation;
- metaphysical grounding;
- creation; and
- physical or information-theoretic correspondence.

These claims have different burdens and can fail separately. The original draft
must be preserved, while its claim families are decomposed into linked papers and
atoms.

### 2. The minimal root must remain minimal

The governing admission is:

> **A0: God Is.**

God is the intended ontological referent. **God Is** is the admitted proposition
or confession pointing to that referent. The sentence must not silently contain
every later Christian doctrine or Theophysics bridge.

### 3. Personhood precedes capability in the reader's journey

The public narrative should encounter God as personal before treating God as a
metaphysical explanatory engine. Human personhood is a doorway into the question,
not a proof that divine personhood is identical to created personhood.

### 4. Identity, constitution, character, capability, and act are distinct

The architecture must distinguish:

| Question | Examples |
|---|---|
| That God is | The admitted root |
| Who God is | Biblical referent; personal; Father, Son, Spirit |
| What is constitutive of God | Aseity; love; truth; life; holiness |
| How divine nature is expressed | Character; Fruits; relational and moral signature |
| What God can do | Know; will; speak; create; sustain; judge; redeem |
| What God has done | Creation; covenant; Incarnation; Cross; Resurrection |
| How God is known | Revelation; proclamation; testimony; reason; created witness |

### 5. Love and truth are not merely optional moral traits

Within the governing Christian reading, **God is love** and **God is truth** are
claims about divine identity or constitution, not merely descriptions of behavior.

Their manifestations may extend beyond ordinary moral vocabulary. The project may
investigate their expression in persons, relations, institutions, information,
and created order, but each cross-domain move remains separately typed and tested.

### 6. Coherence is necessary but not sufficient for moral good

Internal organization alone cannot define morality. Deceptive, exploitative, or
totalitarian systems can be highly coordinated.

The proposed moral-structural account therefore requires at least:

> coherence + truth + orientation toward genuine good + preservation of
> personhood + just relation

This formulation remains a candidate requiring conceptual and adversarial work.

### 7. Character precedes the Fruits bridge

The biblical-theological claim concerning the Fruits must be stated before their
proposed systems interpretation. The Fruits-as-signature model is a conceptual or
structural proposal, not a physical derivation.

Truth should initially be treated as the constitutive frame governing the nine
named Fruits, not casually counted as a tenth Fruit.

### 8. Capability and action must not be collapsed

These are different claims:

- God can create.
- God created the universe.
- The observable universe has a particular physical history.
- That physical history is part of God's creative act.

Each receives its own claim atom, dependencies, evidence class, and boundary.

### 9. Revelation and proclamation are not ordinary derivations

If the root is personal, self-disclosure is an appropriate category for knowing
the source. The proposed category **axiomatic self-proclamation** may be useful,
but claims that a proclamation occurred remain open to textual, historical, and
theological examination.

### 10. Cross-domain recurrence does not establish identity

A bridge must name:

- source and target registers;
- mapped objects;
- preserved structure;
- lost structure;
- boundaries;
- rival mappings;
- grade;
- WHY status; and
- a discriminator or next test.

Physical regularity can be evidence of order and intelligibility without thereby
being identical to divine love. Stronger claims must earn stronger support.

### 11. The Claim Atom system governs the back end, not the story surface

Claims, evidence, proof, and process remain separate objects. The public article
should remain human, sequential, and readable while linking back to the governed
support objects.

### 12. No architecture decision grants canon authority

Folder placement, Git merge, attractive prose, AI agreement, or repeated language
does not admit a claim. Human admission remains a separate governed event.

## Proposed narrative order

> **THAT God is → WHO God is → WHAT is constitutive of God → HOW that nature is
> expressed → WHAT God can do → WHAT God has done → HOW God is known → HOW
> creation may bear the signature → WHAT follows → WHAT rival roots offer.**

This is the current convergence point. The open-rulings ledger records where the
participants have not yet earned agreement.
