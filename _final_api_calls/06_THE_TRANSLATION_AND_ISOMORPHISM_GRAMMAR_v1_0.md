## 1. PURPOSE

Translation begins only after two native objects have been opened and classified under their own burdens. Its job is to say exactly what can be carried from one register to another, what wording remains safe, what is lost, and whether the relation is metaphor, analogy, homomorphism, isomorphism candidate, isomorphism, or identity.

```text
NATIVE A → OPEN A → SIGNATURE A
                         ↘
                          MAPPING → TESTS → BRIDGE RECORD → SAFE WORDING
                         ↗
NATIVE B → OPEN B → SIGNATURE B
```

Translation creates no authority. It preserves and reports authority already earned natively.

## 2. THE THREE-RAIL SENTENCE

Every cross-domain statement must be decomposable into:

```text
NATIVE:         What domain A establishes in its own terms.
BRIDGE:         What structure maps from A to B, with preservation and loss.
IDENTIFICATION: What domain B or Christian theology says the mapped structure means.
```

Safe example:

> Thermodynamics establishes that sustained local restoration requires appropriate openness, work, or input under specified conditions. Theophysics proposes a structural bridge from that source-dependent restoration pattern to creaturely moral repair. Christian theology identifies grace as God’s unearned restorative action; that identification is theological, not a theorem of thermodynamics.

Unsafe example:

> Thermodynamics proves grace.

## 4. GRADE LADDER

```text
INDEPENDENT          — no asserted structural connection.
METAPHORICAL         — evocative language; no preservation claim.
STRUCTURAL_ANALOGY   — selected relations correspond within stated limits.
HOMOMORPHISM         — an explicit one-way structure-preserving map survives tests.
ISOMORPHISM_CANDIDATE — explicit forward/reverse maps appear to preserve and reflect.
STRUCTURAL_ISOMORPHISM — maps, preservation/reflection, dependencies, capabilities,
                         collapse, and translation behavior survive pressure tests.
STRUCTURAL_IDENTITY  — the same structure is demonstrated, not merely mapped.
```

IC-0 through IC-5 separately records depth of demonstrated correspondence. No automatic crosswalk is permitted without a registry.

## 6. TRANSLATION TYPES

### Definition translation

Shows how terms correspond. It fixes vocabulary but proves no factual claim.

### Formal translation

Maps structures, equations, types, relations, or operations. It must state domains, units, assumptions, and equivalence strength.

### Empirical translation

Maps observables and protocols. It requires instruments, calibration, normalization, uncertainty, and validation against native results.

### Historical translation

Maps event, witness, testimony, and interpretation vocabularies. It may not erase transmission uncertainty.

### Theological translation

Maps a discovered pattern into theological language. Scripture, doctrine, and philosophical/historical warrant remain native theological requirements.

### Everyday translation

Renders an admitted object accessibly. It may simplify wording but not grade, scope, or uncertainty.

## 7. SAFE LANGUAGE GENERATOR

Use wording matched to the bridge’s actual grade:

| Demonstrated relation | Licensed wording |
|---|---|
| metaphor | “offers an image of,” “can illustrate” |
| analogy | “resembles in the following respects,” “structurally echoes” |
| one-way homomorphism | “maps into while preserving…” |
| candidate isomorphism | “is an isomorphism candidate under…” |
| admitted isomorphism | “is structurally isomorphic with respect to…” |
| identity | “is the same structure as…” only within the proved scope |
| theological identification | “Christian theology identifies/interprets this as…” |

Every sentence appends or links the loss statement. “Same pattern” is prohibited unless the pattern is enumerated.

## 8. PROOF-PROPAGATION FIREWALL

- Native proof stays native.
- Empirical support stays attached to its protocol and conditions.
- Historical support stays attached to its transmission chain.
- Theology stays theological without apology.
- Analogy never propagates falsification or proof by default.
- An isomorphism may transport a proved structural property only when the property lies inside the demonstrated preserved structure.
- Identification never inherits formal or empirical status from the bridge.
