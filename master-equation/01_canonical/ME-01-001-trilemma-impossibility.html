The Trilemma Impossibility — J + M + W = 3 requires an external cost-bearer

When someone gets hurt, somebody has to pay. If the offender pays fully, that is justice but not mercy. If the offender pays nothing, that is mercy but not justice. You cannot have both in a closed system — the math literally forbids it. The only way to get full justice AND full mercy is if someone from outside the system voluntarily steps in and pays the cost themselves. That is not theology. That is algebra. And it has exactly one historical instantiation.
